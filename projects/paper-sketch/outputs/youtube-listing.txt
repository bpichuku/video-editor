DESCRIPTION (the first two lines show before "Show more", so they carry the hook)


Let's make something. One pencil, one sheet of paper, and a mandala that grows from a tiny dot in the middle. No talking, just the sound of the room and some music.

I drew this over about 15 minutes and sped it up so you can watch the whole thing in under 5. The slow bit near the end is the part I love most: the little flicks with a dot on the end of each one.

What you'll see
0:00  The most satisfying part first
0:09  Start small, in the middle
0:30  Ring after ring, going outwards
1:01  Tiny scallops and dots
1:46  Pointed petals, all the way round
2:32  Shading every petal
3:02  Little flicks with a dot on the end (real time)
3:27  Flicks all the way round
4:02  Last little details
4:18  All done

What I used
A pencil and a sheet of paper. That's it.

If you try your own, I'd love to see it. Tell me in the comments where you started, or which part you found the most calming.
